AppRestarter is now RestinPID

Rest in PID. Same app, new name and identity. Coming from AppRestarter? Download RestinPID, move it to Applications and quit AppRestarter. Your settings and history come across on first launch; switch Accessibility on once for RestinPID.

New in 3.0

  • Menu Bar & Background group: your menu bar and background apps (Paste, KeepingYouAwake, Raycast…) with RAM and CPU, ready to restart or force quit.
  • macOS group: apps that came with macOS (Finder, Dock, Control Centre…) appear when they pass a RAM or CPU limit or stop responding. Limits and visibility are in Settings → General.
  • Send Feedback… in the gear menu: report a problem or suggest a feature.
  • Fewer false “Not Responding” badges for background apps.
  • Restart All and Force Quit All never touch macOS apps; single actions on them always ask first.
