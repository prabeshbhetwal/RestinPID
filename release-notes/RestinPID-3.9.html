RestinPID 3.9

  • The list holds still while you use it. When an app stops responding, Needs Attention no longer pushes the other apps down under your pointer, so a click can't land on the wrong app. New apps wait until your pointer leaves the panel; an orange count at the bottom shows how many.
  • An app that quits while your pointer is over the list keeps its row, dimmed and marked Closed, so the rows below don't jump up.
  • A click right after the list changes is ignored (you'll hear a beep), because it was aimed at the old layout.
  • The right-click menu names the app: "Force Quit Safari", not just "Force Quit".
  • The menu bar icon keeps the same width when it shows a count, so the icons next to it don't shift.
