RestinPID 3.3

  • Totals at a glance: each group heading shows its total RAM and CPU. Or choose Whole Mac to see memory used and CPU load as Activity Monitor shows them. Settings → Usage.
  • Menu bar reading: optionally show RAM and CPU next to the menu bar icon, either for the apps in the list or for the whole Mac. Settings → Usage.
  • Hide quiet apps: show only busy apps. You decide what busy means: over your RAM limit or your CPU limit, or over both at once. Settings → Apps.
  • Restart RestinPID with the new button next to the gear.
  • Tidier Settings: new Apps and Usage tabs, and self-restart has moved to Auto-Restart.
