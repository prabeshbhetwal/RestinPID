RestinPID 3.6

  • Quick hide: a new eye-slash button on each app, next to Restart and Force Quit, takes the app out of the list. Choose 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, until tomorrow, until the app restarts, or always. The same choices are in the right-click menu.
  • Hidden apps are also left out of Restart All and Force Quit All.
  • Hidden for now: Settings → Excluded lists temporarily hidden apps, shows when each one comes back, and has a Show button to bring it back right away.
