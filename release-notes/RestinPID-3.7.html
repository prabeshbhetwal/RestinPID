RestinPID 3.7

  • New icon: a tombstone whose top is a restart arrow, with a heartbeat inside. It replaces the app icon and the menu bar icon.
  • Easier to spot when an app stops responding: the menu bar icon becomes a solid red tombstone with a "!" and a count of frozen apps, a glow pulses behind it, and it jiggles until you open the panel.
  • The warning no longer turns black: it now stays visible on black, white and every menu bar in between.
  • Settings → Alerts: choose the colour, solid or outline, the count, icon size, glow speed, and jiggle strength and timing. Previews show the icon on black, grey and white menu bars, and a button shows it in your menu bar.
  • With Reduce Motion on in System Settings, the glow holds steady and nothing moves.
